Our Heritage

A landmark of the Costa del Sol, in Estepona

Conceived in the late 1980s and completed across five phases, Alcazaba Beach was designed by the renowned architect Melvin Villarroel, whose vision placed human space, the natural environment and Andalusian design tradition at the heart of the community.

Today it remains one of the most prestigious residential estates on the coast: low-rise Mediterranean architecture woven through mature tropical gardens, cascading water features and shaded walkways — all meticulously maintained and just ten minutes from the historic centre of Estepona.

Awards

Alcazaba Beach has received several awards, such as Best Development on the Costa del Sol, Best Community Gardens on the Costa del Sol and the Architectural Heritage Prize for Urbanisations of Environmental Interest, awarded by the Government of Andalucía.

Amenities

Everything a resort offers. Everything a home needs.

Facilities reserved exclusively for owners and residents, cared for year-round by our on-site team.

  • Seven swimming pools

    Pools set throughout the gardens, each serving its own phase and surrounded by lawns and planting.

  • Tennis & padel

    Five tennis courts and three padel courts, with an active community of players all season.

  • Football pitch

    A natural-grass football pitch — a rarity on the coast, loved by children and adults alike.

  • Gym & sauna

    A new, state-of-the-art gymnasium is being built on the front line to the sea, facing directly onto the water — opening in 2027. The sauna is a separate facility in Phase 4.

  • Restaurant & beach bar

    A seasonal restaurant and chiringuito for the exclusive use of owners and their guests.

  • Children's playground

    A dedicated play area set in the gardens for younger residents and their guests.

  • Co-working space

    A shared workspace on site for residents who work remotely.

  • Bicycle storage

    A secure storage room where residents can keep their bicycles.
